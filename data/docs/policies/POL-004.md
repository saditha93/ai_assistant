## Purpose
Crestline Commercial Bank is committed to handling customer complaints fairly, promptly, and transparently. This policy describes how complaints are received, investigated, and resolved, and is published for customer reference.

## How to Complain
Customers may submit a complaint through any branch, the customer service hotline, the mobile banking app, or in writing to the address listed on our website. All complaints are logged with a unique reference number provided to the customer at the time of submission.

## Acknowledgement and Timelines
All complaints are acknowledged within 2 business days. Straightforward complaints are targeted for resolution within 7 business days. Complaints requiring investigation, such as those related to a disputed transaction potentially connected to a service incident, are targeted for resolution within 14 business days, with the customer kept informed of progress at least weekly.

## Investigation
Complaints are investigated by the relevant business unit. Where a complaint relates to a known service disruption, such as a payment failure event, the investigation team may reference internal incident records to confirm timing and scope, though the underlying technical detail is not shared externally beyond what is relevant to the customer's specific case.

## Compensation
Where Crestline Commercial Bank determines that a service failure directly caused customer loss, such as a fee incorrectly charged during a payment processing incident, affected customers are compensated according to our standard goodwill framework. Customers do not need to prove fault; a reasonable connection between the service issue and the customer's loss is sufficient.

## Escalation
If a customer is not satisfied with the outcome of their complaint, they may request escalation to a senior complaints officer, and subsequently may refer the matter to the applicable external financial ombudsman or regulator.

## Reporting
Complaint volumes and root cause trends are reviewed monthly by retail leadership and, where a complaint pattern correlates with a technology incident, are shared with the relevant engineering team to support the incident review process described internally in POL-003.
