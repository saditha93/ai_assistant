## Purpose and Scope
This policy establishes baseline information security requirements for all Crestline Commercial Bank employees, contractors, and systems. It applies to all environments, including production systems described in ARCH-001 through ARCH-004, and to all documents classified under POL-002.

## Access Control
Access to systems and data must follow the principle of least privilege. Access to restricted-classification systems, such as the key management service described in ARCH-004, requires two-person authorisation and documented business justification. All access grants are reviewed quarterly by the security team, and access to production payment systems is reviewed monthly given its risk profile.

## Logging and Monitoring
Application logs must not contain unredacted card numbers, national identity card numbers, or other customer PII, per the data classification rules in POL-002. Any discovery of unredacted sensitive data in logs, such as identified in INC-2026-021, must be reported to the security team within 24 hours and remediated on a prioritised basis.

## Vulnerability and Patch Management
Critical vulnerabilities must be patched within 7 days of disclosure for internet-facing systems and 30 days for internal systems. Certificate and key lifecycle management, including the gaps identified in INC-2026-005, falls under this policy and is operationalised through RB-003.

## Incident Reporting
Any suspected security incident, including but not limited to unauthorised access, data exposure, or compromised credentials, must be reported immediately to the security team and will be triaged under POL-003. Suspected key material compromise is treated as a minimum SEV1 event regardless of confirmed impact.

## Third-Party Risk
Vendors with access to customer data or critical infrastructure, such as NorthGate Card Switch, must undergo an annual security review. Vendor incident coordination procedures are documented in service-specific runbooks such as RB-004.

## Review and Exceptions
This policy is reviewed annually by the security team. Exceptions require written approval from the CISO, Amali Fernando, and must be time-bound with a documented remediation plan.
