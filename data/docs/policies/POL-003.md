## Purpose
This policy defines how incidents affecting Crestline Commercial Bank's technology services are classified, escalated, and reviewed. All incident documents in this knowledge base, including INC-2025-041 and INC-2026-021, follow this policy's structure and severity definitions.

## Severity Definitions
SEV1: critical, bank-wide or near-bank-wide customer impact, such as authorisation success rate below 70% or a core service fully unavailable. Requires immediate incident commander assignment and executive notification within 30 minutes. SEV2: significant but partial impact, such as a regional degradation or a feature-specific outage affecting a meaningful customer segment. SEV3: minor or contained impact, often caught before significant customer-facing effect, or affecting a small customer segment.

## Response Times
SEV1 incidents require acknowledgement within 5 minutes of paging and a status update every 30 minutes until resolution. SEV2 incidents require acknowledgement within 15 minutes and updates every hour. SEV3 incidents require acknowledgement within 1 business hour.

## Roles
Every declared incident requires an incident owner, typically the on-call engineer for the affected service per services.json, responsible for technical resolution, and for SEV1/SEV2 incidents, a separate incident commander responsible for coordination and communication, distinct from the technical owner.

## Post-Incident Review
A post-incident review is required for all SEV1 and SEV2 incidents within 5 business days of resolution. The review must document root cause, timeline, customer impact, and action items with named owners. Recurring root causes, such as the payments-ledger-db capacity pattern seen across INC-2025-019, INC-2025-041, and INC-2026-013, must be explicitly flagged and escalated to the relevant architecture review board, see MTG-2026-009, if unresolved after a second occurrence.

## Action Item Follow-Up
Action items are tracked to completion and reviewed at the relevant team's weekly sync, for payments see MTG-2026-014. An action item that remains incomplete across two or more incidents of the same root cause category is escalated to the department head automatically.

## Communication
Customer-facing communication for SEV1 and SEV2 incidents is coordinated with the retail and digital teams per the principles in POL-004, even when the underlying incident is not customer-service-initiated.
