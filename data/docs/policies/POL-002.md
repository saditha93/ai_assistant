## Purpose
This policy defines the four data and document classification levels used across Crestline Commercial Bank and the handling requirements for each. It governs the access_level applied to every internal document, including all documents referenced throughout this knowledge base.

## Classification Levels
Public: information approved for external release with no restriction, such as POL-004. Internal: information intended for Crestline employees and contractors only, not for external distribution; this is the default level for most operational documents including incident reports, architecture documents, and runbooks. Confidential: information with material business sensitivity, such as specific merchant financial exposure (INC-2025-052) or committee-level risk assessments (MTG-2026-016), requiring need-to-know access. Restricted: the highest classification, covering key management design (ARCH-004) and similar material where disclosure could materially affect the bank's security posture.

## Handling Requirements
Confidential and restricted documents must not be forwarded outside their originating department without owner approval. Confidential documents may be discussed in cross-departmental meetings only when necessary for incident resolution or governance, as occurred when INC-2025-052 was referenced during architecture review. Restricted documents require named, logged access.

## Customer Data Specifically
Card numbers, NIC numbers, account numbers, and biometric identifiers are always treated as at least confidential regardless of the containing document's overall classification, and must be masked or tokenised in any log, report, or ticket. The unredacted log excerpt found during INC-2026-021 was a direct violation of this requirement and was escalated for remediation.

## Retention
Incident reports are retained for a minimum of 3 years. Architecture and policy documents are retained indefinitely with version history. Meeting notes are retained for 2 years unless they document a governance decision, such as MTG-2026-009, in which case they are retained indefinitely alongside the related architecture document.

## Enforcement
Misclassification or mishandling of documents under this policy is addressed through the standard security incident process in POL-001. Repeated or wilful violations may result in disciplinary action coordinated with HR.
