## Summary
SEV2. On 2025-05-20, between 11:40 and 13:15 IST, the CrestPay gateway returned elevated 5xx and timeout errors for card and instant transfer payments during the Vesak long-weekend shopping peak. Root service: payments-ledger-db (see ARCH-001). Approximately 4,200 customers experienced at least one failed transaction, and around 6,800 transactions were declined or timed out before being retried successfully.

## Impact
Card authorisation success rate on CrestPay dropped from a normal 99.4% to as low as 71% for 95 minutes. QR merchant payments (SPEC-002) were also affected because they share the same orchestrator-to-ledger path. No funds were lost or double-debited; all affected transactions either failed cleanly or were reconciled the same day.

## Timeline
- 11:40 - Alerting fired on elevated payment-orchestrator error rate.
- 11:52 - On-call engineer Kasun Rajapaksa paged; confirmed payments-ledger-db connection pool at 100% utilisation.
- 12:05 - Incident declared SEV2, Nimal Perera as incident owner.
- 12:30 - Idle connections manually recycled; pool briefly recovered then re-saturated under continued load.
- 13:05 - Non-critical batch reporting jobs against the ledger DB paused, freeing connections.
- 13:15 - Error rate returned to baseline.

## Root Cause
The payments-ledger-db connection pool (max 150 connections) was sized for average daily volume, not long-weekend peaks. A batch reconciliation job that normally runs overnight was still executing during the peak window and held a disproportionate number of connections, starving live transaction traffic.

## Resolution
Connections were freed by pausing the batch job; traffic recovered without a service restart. A temporary increase in max pool size to 200 was applied as a stopgap pending a permanent fix.

## Action Items

- Move batch reconciliation jobs to a dedicated read replica (owner: Kasun Rajapaksa) - not completed.
- Review connection pool sizing methodology against peak-day volumes rather than daily averages (owner: Dinesh Wickramasinghe) - not completed.
