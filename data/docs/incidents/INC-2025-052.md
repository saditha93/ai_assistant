## Summary
SEV1. On 2025-11-28 (Black Friday), NorthGate Card Switch (see ARCH-001) began responding to authorisation requests with elevated latency starting at 12:05 IST. Because the payment-orchestrator had no circuit breaker and retried aggressively on timeout, the retry traffic itself overwhelmed NorthGate's inbound capacity, extending the outage well beyond NorthGate's own initial degradation. Approximately 22,000 customers were affected over 2 hours 45 minutes; an estimated 58,000 transactions failed, timed out, or were duplicated at the orchestrator layer before dedupe logic caught them.

## Impact
Card-present and card-not-present authorisations both failed intermittently. Several large merchant partners reported cart abandonment spikes and financial exposure well above typical incident levels. This document is marked confidential due to the scale of merchant financial exposure discussed with Legal and the Head of Risk.

## Timeline
- 12:05 - NorthGate latency increases from approximately 200ms to 4-6 seconds per call.
- 12:08 - payment-orchestrator begins retrying timed-out calls up to 3 times per transaction with no backoff.
- 12:15 - NorthGate's inbound queue saturates under retry volume; NorthGate's own availability drops further.
- 12:30 - SEV1 declared, Ruwan Gunasekara as incident owner, James O'Brien engaged as NorthGate vendor liaison.
- 13:10 - Orchestrator retries manually disabled via feature flag.
- 14:20 - NorthGate confirms recovery on their side.
- 14:50 - Full service restored and verified.

## Root Cause
NorthGate experienced a capacity event on their end, per their incident report shared under NDA. The absence of exponential backoff and a circuit breaker in the payment-orchestrator's NorthGate client converted a moderate vendor slowdown into a self-inflicted retry storm that outlasted the original cause. See RB-004.

## Resolution
Retries were disabled via feature flag and traffic throttled until NorthGate confirmed recovery. Retry logic was re-enabled afterward with a temporary hard cap of one retry per transaction.

## Action Items
- Implement exponential backoff and a circuit breaker for all NorthGate Card Switch calls (owner: Ruwan Gunasekara) - not completed at the time of this incident, carried forward.
- Establish a shared incident bridge protocol with NorthGate for SEV1/SEV2 events (owner: James O'Brien) - completed 2026-01-15.
- Add per-downstream retry budget limits to the orchestrator's HTTP client library (owner: Thilina Bandara) - not completed.

See INC-2026-021 for a recurrence of this failure mode after the circuit breaker was implemented, and MTG-2026-009 for the architecture review board decision approving that work.
