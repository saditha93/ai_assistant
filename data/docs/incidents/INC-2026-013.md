## Summary
SEV2. On 2026-04-13, during the Sinhala and Tamil New Year shopping and remittance peak, payments-ledger-db again approached connection pool exhaustion between 10:00 and 11:10 IST. Approximately 11,200 customers experienced at least one failed or delayed transaction; the incident was contained faster than prior occurrences due to alerting implemented after INC-2025-041.

## Impact
Card authorisation success rate dipped to 84% at the worst point, a smaller drop than INC-2025-019 or INC-2025-041 due to the read-replica migration completed earlier in 2026. CrestPay Instant transfers (SPEC-001) saw brief latency increases but no outright failures.

## Timeline
- 09:52 - Automated alert fires at 80% connection pool utilisation, implemented as an action item from INC-2025-041.
- 09:55 - Kasun Rajapaksa engages proactively, ahead of any customer-facing errors.
- 10:00 - Pool utilisation crosses 92%; first authorisation failures observed.
- 10:15 - SEV2 declared.
- 10:35 - Additional read-only connections rerouted to the replica per RB-002.
- 11:10 - Pool utilisation returns below 70%; incident closed.

## Root Cause
Despite the 2025 migration of batch and reporting workloads to a read replica, a subset of internal dashboards used by the fraud engine (see ARCH-001) still queried the primary ledger database directly. Combined with New Year peak transaction volume of approximately 2.9x average, this was sufficient to approach pool exhaustion again, though the proactive alert meant the incident was caught before a full outage.

## Resolution
Fraud dashboard queries were redirected to the replica during the incident and pool utilisation normalised. No emergency pool resize was required this time.

## Action Items
- Migrate all remaining direct-to-primary read queries, including fraud engine dashboards, to the read replica (owner: Kasun Rajapaksa) - completed 2026-05-01, closing the action item first raised in INC-2025-019.
- Establish a permanent peak-season capacity review two weeks ahead of known high-volume dates (owner: Dinesh Wickramasinghe) - completed, first review held 2026-06-01, see MTG-2026-014.
- Evaluate horizontal read scaling for payments-ledger-db beyond a single replica (owner: Kasun Rajapaksa) - in progress.

This is the third recorded occurrence of ledger database saturation following INC-2025-019 and INC-2025-041; see RB-002 for the standing response procedure.
