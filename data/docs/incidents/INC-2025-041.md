## Summary
SEV1. On 2025-10-24 (month-end salary disbursement day), CrestPay card authorisations and instant transfers failed intermittently between 09:10 and 11:30 IST. Root service: payments-ledger-db, exhausted connection pool under peak load, the same failure class as INC-2025-019. Approximately 15,800 customers were affected; an estimated 41,000 transactions failed or timed out.

## Impact
Card authorisation success rate fell to 58% at the worst point. CrestPay Instant transfers (SPEC-001) and retail branch POS terminals were both affected. Call centre volume tripled and three corporate payroll clients escalated directly to their relationship managers.

## Timeline
- 09:10 - payment-orchestrator error rate alert fires; on-call Priyanka Jayasuriya engages.
- 09:22 - payments-ledger-db connection pool confirmed at 100% (150/150 connections in use).
- 09:30 - SEV1 declared, Nimal Perera as incident owner, Chamari Silva as incident commander.
- 09:45 - Non-essential reporting queries against the ledger DB killed per RB-002.
- 10:15 - Emergency pool size increase to 250 applied; partial recovery observed.
- 11:05 - Read-heavy analytics jobs rerouted away from the primary; full recovery.
- 11:30 - Incident closed, monitoring continued through end of day.

## Root Cause
Salary day transaction volume was roughly 3.4x average daily volume. The temporary pool size increase applied after INC-2025-019 (200) was insufficient, and the connection pool alerting action item from that incident had not been implemented, so on-call staff again diagnosed the saturation manually rather than being paged proactively.

## Resolution
Pool size raised to 250 as an emergency change; reporting workload isolated from the transactional path. Full service was restored within 140 minutes of detection.

## Action Items
- Implement automated connection pool utilisation alerting at 80% threshold (owner: Kasun Rajapaksa) - carried over from INC-2025-019, still not completed at the time of this incident.
- Move batch and reporting workloads permanently to a read replica (owner: Kasun Rajapaksa) - in progress.
- Re-architect payment-orchestrator to use a bounded connection pool with request queuing rather than failing open (owner: Thilina Bandara) - not completed.
- Document a salary-day and festive-season capacity plan referencing RB-002 (owner: Dinesh Wickramasinghe) - completed 2025-11-10.

See RB-002 for the standard database connection pool exhaustion response procedure and INC-2026-013 for the next recurrence of this pattern.
