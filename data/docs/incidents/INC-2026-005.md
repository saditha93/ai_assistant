## Summary
SEV2. On 2026-02-10 at 00:03 IST, the TLS certificate for the CrestPay API gateway expired, causing all external clients (mobile app, merchant integrations, partner banks) to fail TLS handshake. Approximately 9,700 customers and an unknown number of merchant integrations were affected over 80 minutes before the certificate was renewed and the gateway reloaded.

## Impact
All CrestPay traffic requiring a fresh TLS handshake failed with certificate errors; established long-lived connections continued to function until recycled, which limited the blast radius somewhat. Mobile banking (ARCH-003) transactions routed through CrestPay were also affected.

## Timeline
- 00:03 - Certificate expires; client-side TLS errors begin appearing in merchant integration logs.
- 00:18 - Internal monitoring shows a spike in gateway 5xx and connection errors, but no dedicated certificate-expiry alert existed at the time.
- 00:41 - A merchant partner's integration team emails support asking why their integration is failing; ticket escalated to on-call.
- 00:55 - Priyanka Jayasuriya confirms the expired certificate as root cause.
- 01:10 - New certificate issued and deployed; gateway processes reloaded.
- 01:23 - Recovery verified across all regions.

## Root Cause
The certificate had a 12-month validity period and was not covered by the automated renewal tooling used for other internal services; it was tracked in a spreadsheet maintained by the previous certificate owner, who left the company in late 2025 without formal handover of this specific responsibility. No expiry alert existed for this particular certificate.

## Resolution
A new certificate was issued through the standard CA process and deployed manually. The gateway was reloaded rather than restarted, minimising additional downtime.

## Action Items
- Add automated certificate expiry monitoring with alerts at 30, 14, and 3 days before expiry for all externally facing certificates (owner: Priyanka Jayasuriya) - completed 2026-02-24, see RB-003.
- Migrate all payments-domain certificates to the automated renewal pipeline used elsewhere in the platform (owner: Priyanka Jayasuriya) - completed 2026-03-15.
- Audit remaining manually tracked certificates bank-wide for similar gaps (owner: Amali Fernando) - completed 2026-04-01, see POL-001.
