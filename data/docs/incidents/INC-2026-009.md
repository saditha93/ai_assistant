## Summary
SEV3. On 2026-05-18 between 07:15 and 10:35 IST, 42 ATMs in the Central Province (Kandy and surrounding towns) experienced intermittent connectivity loss to the atm-network switch, resulting in failed cash withdrawals and balance enquiries. Approximately 6,400 customers attempted transactions at affected terminals during the window; around 1,900 transactions failed outright.

## Impact
Affected ATMs displayed "unable to process" or timed out entirely. No cash was dispensed without a corresponding debit, and no double-debits were identified during reconciliation. Core banking (ARCH-002) itself was not affected; the issue was isolated to the regional network link.

## Timeline
- 07:15 - Regional network link to the Kandy data closet begins flapping.
- 07:40 - Monitoring flags an elevated ATM transaction failure rate for the Central Province cluster.
- 08:05 - David Fernandes engages network operations and the telecom carrier.
- 08:30 - SEV3 declared.
- 09:20 - Carrier identifies a fibre degradation event affecting the regional link.
- 10:10 - Traffic failed over to the backup microwave link.
- 10:35 - Full service restored across all 42 terminals.

## Root Cause
A third-party telecom carrier fibre link serving the Kandy regional data closet degraded due to a reported construction-related cable disturbance nearby. Automatic failover to the backup microwave link was configured but had a longer-than-expected detection threshold (45 minutes), which delayed recovery.

## Resolution
Manual failover was triggered once the carrier confirmed the fibre issue; service was restored on the backup link within 25 minutes of the manual trigger.

## Action Items
- Reduce automatic failover detection threshold for regional ATM network links from 45 to 10 minutes (owner: David Fernandes) - completed 2026-06-02.
- Add a secondary independent carrier for the Kandy regional link (owner: David Fernandes) - in progress, target 2026-12-01.
- Publish an updated regional network failover runbook (owner: Chathura Abeysekara) - not completed.

Retail operations tracks ATM availability metrics as part of the quarterly incident trend reporting described in POL-003.
