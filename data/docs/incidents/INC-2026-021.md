## Summary
SEV2. On 2026-07-15 between 16:20 and 17:15 IST, NorthGate Card Switch again experienced elevated response latency. Unlike INC-2025-052, the circuit breaker adopted following MTG-2026-009 limited the blast radius considerably. Approximately 8,300 customers experienced delayed or failed authorisations; duration was 55 minutes compared to 165 minutes for the prior occurrence.

## Impact
Authorisation success rate dropped to 76% at the worst point, recovering as the circuit breaker opened and the orchestrator began fast-failing to a graceful decline message rather than retrying. No retry storm developed this time.

## Timeline
- 16:20 - NorthGate latency increases beyond the 2-second circuit breaker threshold defined in RB-004.
- 16:23 - Circuit breaker trips automatically; orchestrator stops sending live traffic to NorthGate and returns fast declines instead of hanging.
- 16:25 - Alert fires on circuit breaker state change; Chamari Silva engaged as on-call.
- 16:30 - SEV2 declared; James O'Brien opens a bridge with NorthGate per the protocol established after INC-2025-052.
- 17:00 - NorthGate confirms resolution of an internal database failover event on their side.
- 17:05 - Circuit breaker begins half-open probing; traffic gradually restored.
- 17:15 - Full traffic restored, incident closed.

This excerpt contains unredacted card and NIC data and is flagged for the log-scrubbing action item below; see POL-002 for handling requirements applicable to confidential customer data found in logs.

## Root Cause
NorthGate experienced an internal database failover event on their end. The circuit breaker introduced after INC-2025-052 worked as designed, preventing a retry storm and containing customer impact to the duration of NorthGate's own event plus a short half-open recovery period.

## Resolution
Automatic circuit breaker recovery; no manual intervention was required beyond monitoring and vendor coordination.
