## Attendees
Nimal Perera (chair), Ruwan Gunasekara, Thilina Bandara, Kasun Rajapaksa, Priyanka Jayasuriya, Chamari Silva, Sarah O'Connell.

## Agenda
Weekly payments engineering sync. This week's focus: review of recurring incident patterns ahead of the upcoming Q3 architecture review, and status of outstanding action items from INC-2026-013 and INC-2026-021.

## Discussion
Kasun Rajapaksa presented an updated capacity model for payments-ledger-db, noting that the read replica migration completed following INC-2026-013 has held utilisation below 70% even during subsequent moderate peaks. The group agreed the database connection pool pattern that caused INC-2025-019, INC-2025-041, and INC-2026-013 is now considered largely mitigated, though horizontal read scaling remains a longer-term item.

Priyanka Jayasuriya raised that the log redaction action item from INC-2026-021, removing raw card and NIC data from orchestrator debug logs, is still not completed and is now the longest-outstanding open action item across the payments incident history. The group agreed to escalate this to Dinesh Wickramasinghe given it has now missed two internal target dates.

Thilina Bandara confirmed the config validation guardrails from INC-2025-047 remain in place and no similar configuration incidents have occurred since.

Sarah O'Connell asked whether CrestPay Instant transfers (SPEC-001) roadmap items should be paused pending confirmation that peak capacity is stable; the group agreed to proceed but to re-run a peak load test before the next festive season.

## Decisions
The log redaction action item is escalated to high priority with a hard deadline of 2026-09-30. A peak load test for payments-ledger-db and payment-orchestrator will be scheduled before the next Sinhala and Tamil New Year period. Horizontal read scaling evaluation for payments-ledger-db will be brought to the next architecture review board.

## Next Steps
Priyanka Jayasuriya to provide a firm completion date for log redaction by 2026-08-27. Kasun Rajapaksa to draft the horizontal read scaling proposal for architecture review. Nimal Perera to update the recurring incident tracker referenced in the POL-003 post-incident review guidance.
