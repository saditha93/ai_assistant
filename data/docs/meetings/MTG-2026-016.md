## Attendees
Ganesh Moorthy (chair), Amali Fernando, Lakshan Perera, Dinesh Wickramasinghe, Rohan Wickremesinghe (Non-Executive Director, Risk Committee).

## Agenda
Quarterly risk committee review covering technology incident trends, the Platinum credit card rewards programme cost overrun, and operational risk items flagged by internal audit.

## Discussion
Dinesh Wickramasinghe presented a summary of the payments platform incident history for the quarter, highlighting that the recurring payments-ledger-db capacity pattern (INC-2025-019, INC-2025-041, INC-2026-013) is now considered substantially mitigated following the read replica migration, while the outstanding log redaction gap from INC-2026-021 remains an open operational risk item pending closure by 2026-09-30 per MTG-2026-014.

Lakshan Perera presented the Platinum credit card rewards programme (SPEC-003) cost position, confirming the programme is running at 1.24% of eligible spend against a 1.1% target, driven by higher than forecast lounge access utilisation. The committee requested a firm remediation plan, including the proposed lounge access cap, be brought to the Q4 meeting with a quantified cost impact.

Amali Fernando reported no confirmed security incidents in the quarter meeting the threshold for board notification, though she flagged the unredacted PII found in application logs during INC-2026-021 as a near-miss worth tracking until fully remediated, given the potential regulatory exposure had it reached an external system.

Rohan Wickremesinghe asked whether the frequency of payment-related incidents this year represented an increasing trend; Dinesh Wickramasinghe clarified that while incident count is roughly flat year over year, severity and customer impact have trended down due to the resilience improvements, including the circuit breaker, read replica, and alerting, implemented following the earlier incidents.

## Decisions
The Platinum rewards programme remediation plan is required for the Q4 committee meeting. The log redaction item is tracked as a near-miss risk item until closure. No board notification is required for security incidents this quarter.

## Next Steps
Lakshan Perera to bring a quantified remediation plan to Q4. Amali Fernando to confirm closure of the log redaction item and report back to the committee outside the regular cycle if not closed by 2026-09-30. Dinesh Wickramasinghe to continue quarterly incident trend reporting.
