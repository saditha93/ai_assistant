## Attendees
James O'Brien (Crestline, chair), Ruwan Gunasekara (Crestline), Emily Watson (NorthGate, Account Director), Marcus Lee (NorthGate, Technical Lead).

## Agenda
Quarterly vendor sync with NorthGate Card Switch covering recent incident history, the circuit breaker integration approved at MTG-2026-009, and upcoming NorthGate infrastructure changes that may affect Crestline traffic.

## Notes

Marcus Lee confirmed NorthGate's database failover process, involved in the more recent INC-2026-021, has since been tuned to complete in under 2 minutes rather than the roughly 40 minutes observed in that incident.

Ruwan Gunasekara shared that the circuit breaker on Crestline's side performed as expected during INC-2026-021, containing customer impact despite the underlying NorthGate event. Both sides agreed the shared incident bridge process, established after INC-2025-052, is working well and should remain the standard escalation path for future SEV1/SEV2 events involving both parties.
