## Attendees
Michael Chen (chair), Dinesh Wickramasinghe, Ruwan Gunasekara, Thilina Bandara, Amali Fernando, Suresh Kumar, James O'Brien.

## Agenda
Review of the payment-orchestrator's resilience patterns for third-party integrations, prompted by the retry storm in INC-2025-052 and the recommendation from that incident's action items. Secondary agenda item: configuration deployment safeguards following INC-2025-047.

## Discussion
Ruwan Gunasekara presented the proposed circuit breaker design for the NorthGate Card Switch client, including trip thresholds (2 second average latency or 20% error rate over a 10-second window), half-open probing behaviour, and manual override procedures. Michael Chen noted this pattern should become the standard for all external dependency calls from the payment-orchestrator, not just NorthGate, and asked for a follow-up proposal covering other third-party integrations within the payments domain.

James O'Brien confirmed NorthGate had no objection to the half-open probing approach and agreed to formalise a shared incident bridge process, which was subsequently implemented as an action item from INC-2025-052.

Amali Fernando raised that circuit breaker state changes should be logged and auditable given their operational significance, which was accepted as a requirement.

On configuration safeguards, Thilina Bandara summarised the mandatory peer review and staging soak process introduced after INC-2025-047, and the board agreed this should extend to all production configuration changes across the payments domain, not just the orchestrator, with implementation tracked separately by each service owner.

## Decisions
The circuit breaker design for NorthGate Card Switch is approved for implementation, target completion end of Q2 2026, and is documented operationally in RB-004. The peer review and staging soak requirement for configuration changes is extended bank-wide across payments domain services.

## Next Steps
Ruwan Gunasekara to implement and validate the circuit breaker, later confirmed effective in INC-2026-021. Suresh Kumar to assess applicability of the circuit breaker pattern to core banking integration calls. Michael Chen to bring a broader third-party resilience proposal to the next quarterly review.
