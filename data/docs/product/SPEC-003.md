## Overview
The Platinum credit card rewards programme offers accelerated points earning, airport lounge access, and preferential foreign exchange rates to Crestline's highest-tier credit card customers. This specification is marked confidential as it contains commercial terms and margin information not intended for general internal distribution.

## Earning Structure
Cardholders earn 4 points per LKR 100 spent on international transactions, 2 points per LKR 100 on local retail, and 1 point per LKR 100 on all other spend categories. Points do not expire while the account remains active and in good standing.

## Redemption
Points may be redeemed for airline miles at a rate of 1 point to 1 mile with select partner airlines, for statement credit at a lower effective rate, or for merchandise through the rewards catalogue. Redemption processing integrates with core-banking (ARCH-002) for statement credit application.

## Cost and Margin
The programme's fully loaded cost, including partner airline mile purchase cost and lounge access fees, is targeted not to exceed 1.1% of eligible spend. Current actual cost as of Q2 2026 is running at 1.24%, above target, primarily due to higher than forecast lounge access utilisation; this was flagged to the Q3 risk committee, see MTG-2026-016.

## Eligibility
Eligibility requires a minimum annual income threshold and a Crestline relationship of at least 12 months, or an equivalent balance held in other Crestline products. Existing Gold tier cardholders are eligible for automatic upgrade review after 18 months of qualifying spend.

## Risk Considerations
The rewards liability is recognised as a financial liability on the balance sheet and is reviewed quarterly by finance and risk jointly. The cost overrun noted above is being addressed through a proposed lounge access cap, expected to be finalised in Q4 2026.
