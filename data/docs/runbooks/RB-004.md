## Purpose
This runbook covers the response to degraded availability or elevated latency from NorthGate Card Switch, the external card network switch integration described in ARCH-001. It was formalised after the circuit breaker adoption decision at MTG-2026-009, following the retry storm in INC-2025-052.

## Detection
The payment-orchestrator's NorthGate client monitors rolling latency and error rate; the circuit breaker trips automatically when average latency exceeds 2 seconds over a 10-second window, or error rate exceeds 20% over the same window. A circuit breaker state-change alert fires to the payments on-call.

## Steps
1. On a circuit breaker trip alert, confirm the breaker is open, meaning it is rejecting fast rather than hanging, by checking the orchestrator dashboard.
2. Open a bridge with NorthGate's operations team using the shared incident contact process established after INC-2025-052.
3. Monitor the half-open probe rate; the breaker automatically sends a small percentage of live traffic through periodically to test recovery.
4. Do not manually force the circuit closed while NorthGate has not confirmed resolution, as this risks recreating the retry storm seen in INC-2025-052.
5. Once NorthGate confirms resolution and probe traffic succeeds consistently for 5 minutes, the breaker closes automatically.

## Escalation
Declare SEV2 if the breaker remains open for more than 30 minutes, or SEV1 if NorthGate cannot provide an estimated recovery time within 45 minutes. James O'Brien is the primary vendor liaison for all NorthGate-related incidents, see MTG-2026-011 for the current vendor contact context.

## Rollback
There is no manual rollback step required under normal operation since the circuit breaker is self-healing. If the circuit breaker itself malfunctions, either failing to trip when it should or failing to recover when NorthGate is healthy, disable automatic breaker logic via feature flag and fall back to the manual retry-disable procedure used during INC-2025-052 while the breaker is investigated.
